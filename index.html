About

Academic profile

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at CUET. My research focuses on intelligent systems for healthcare, safety, and infrastructure monitoring. My undergraduate thesis explored skin lesion segmentation using transfer learning, while my current MSc research investigates a multi-modal deep learning framework for road damage detection.
